💡 GMP is low — what should you do?
GMP is unofficial and can be pushed up or down by operators — never decide on GMP alone. Use this 2-check rule:
• GMP low + QIB strong (10x+): institutions see value the grey market is missing. This can be a quiet good apply — check valuation and decide.
• GMP low + QIB weak (below 2x): both signals agree there is no demand. Skip it — this is where most listing losses happen.
• GMP high + QIB weak: hype trap. Retail excitement without institutional money often lists okay and falls fast, or falls on day 1 itself.
• GMP high + QIB strong: the classic strong IPO (like BLS) — apply, and plan your exit with the Sell tab.
